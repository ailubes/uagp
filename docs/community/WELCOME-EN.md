# Welcome to UAGP

**UAGP (Ukrainian Algorithmic Governance Protocol)** is an open civic research and engineering initiative exploring how citizens can continuously participate in improving governance in Ukraine.

UAGP is **not** an official government service, a political party, an electoral platform, or an autonomous AI government. Proposals shared here do not become legal petitions or administrative applications. Models cannot receive sovereign authority or change their own permissions.

## How you can help

- **Residents and community organizers:** describe a concrete local problem and the change you want to see. A technical solution is not required.
- **Legal and constitutional experts:** review assumptions about local government, constitutional rights, consent and lawful authority.
- **Economists and researchers:** examine public evidence, costs, trade-offs and evaluation methodology.
- **Developers, security engineers and designers:** help design open protocols, safe citizen interfaces and portable AI adapters.
- **Translators and accessibility specialists:** improve plain-language Ukrainian participation and remove digital barriers.

[Start here in Ukrainian](START-HERE.md) · [Community principles](PRINCIPLES.md) · [Public issue tracker](https://github.com/ailubes/uagp/issues) · [Open Charter overview](../charter.md) · [Contributor guidelines](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).

GitHub can be read without an account but requires one for issues and comments. An independent, self-hosted UAGP identity provider and a non-GitHub citizen participation channel are planned, **not deployed**. AI subscriptions are optional: citizens should be able to participate manually or bring their own compatible model.

## Core commitments

Citizen needs drive development, but citizens remain the legitimate source of political authority. All technical changes are contestable and evidence-based, with explicit human approval, legal limits and privacy safeguards. Disagreement and minority perspectives matter. Participation must not depend on any private provider, including Wesfin; the proposed identity layer will be independently operated.

The full documentation is written primarily in Ukrainian. Contributions in English are welcome with an effort to provide accessible Ukrainian summaries.
